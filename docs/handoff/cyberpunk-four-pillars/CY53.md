# CY53 Story authoring · Life aboard and civilian management feedback

## Outcome and dependencies

Give the management screens and location art coherent, concise campaign context. Use the
[delivery contract](delivery-contract.md), [CY51 Development](https://github.com/luismlorenzana/uma-cyberpunk/blob/main/docs/handoff/tasks/CY51.md) and
[CY52 Art](https://github.com/luismlorenzana/uma-cyberpunk/blob/main/docs/handoff/tasks/CY52.md). Read the current private ship, world and service bibles. P1's meeting, cabin
and morning staging is the common starting point.

## Authoring work

1. Reconcile the ship's rooms with actual reachable campaign entries. For each room, record its
   identity, function, visual anchors, who can physically be there and the existing scene/panel IDs.
   Mark a proposed interaction as a development request. Art does not imply a new working feature.
2. Write short first-use context for supply exports, local reserves, ship upkeep, project/refit
   completion and equipment preparation where existing scenes need it. Explain the player-visible
   consequence through crew activity and a concrete prop. Keep routine amounts/costs generated from
   the game; avoid hard-coding values into dialogue that the ledger can change.
3. Bind the first real shipment to the Hangar and its funded fitting/refit. Specify the cargo, who
   receives it, any single-use acknowledgement and the destination of the material. Give CY52 the
   exact facility, cargo and handover states that must be visually distinct.
4. Cover shortage relief, unfinished work and an unavailable officer with brief coherent responses.
   State whether a beat occurs once, repeats, or changes after a project. Keep the game's nightly
   order and actual costs unchanged unless a mechanic request is agreed with CY51.
5. Preserve civilian control, local work and resettled officers' contributions after liberation.
   Review later-tier ferry, school, receiver and arrival services against the shared room/prop kit.
   Distinct local services can share a location plate while retaining their own words and props.
6. Keep dating, custody and command appointments independent. A commander in local custody is not
   casually present in a ship lounge. Reconcile travel and physical presence with P1 visits and P3
   station calls. An image replacement must not introduce a new plot gate.

## Handoff

Return checked private script/bible revisions, scene IDs, occurrence/availability conditions,
speaker and expression cues, desired room/state assets and a list of new hook requests. For each
management state, identify what the player should understand before and after acting. State which
information comes from the live ledger and which is narrative text.

Use the shared record to name the matching CY52 source version and CY51 runtime fixture. Supply
short draft lines early so development's first real-campaign checkpoint can run. Retain approved
earlier story and provide a scoped change list with the export.

## Verification and completion

Read the lines over the actual cargo, workshop, project and room views. Play a funded fitting/refit,
one insufficient-resource path, a nightly completion and a resumed pending project. Check a later
intake and an ashore officer's contribution. Run the current story/game pack checks; record any
unsupported condition before final authoring.

Done when the agreed management state inventory has coherent checked context, all once-only effects
and physical-presence conditions are explicit, and the complete prep sequence plays with the matching
art. Record remaining phone observations in CY37 and delete this packet with its Done row.
