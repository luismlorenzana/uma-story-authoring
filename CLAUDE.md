# Story authoring rules

Read and follow AGENTS.md before any repository work. Its owner-private art restrictions apply to Claude and
all other AI sessions, across local storage, other repos, cloud URLs, browsers, archives and caches.

Never view, search, render, OCR, attach or extract metadata/text from private images, prompts, editing notes,
generation histories or actual approval records. Never inspect owner-private/, owner-only/, private-variants/,
private-art-outbox/, art-private/, files containing .private., or the old personal studio. Keep private game
views off during agent work. Normal story references and sandbox views use normal art only.

Only the documented count-only private-art commands in uma-art-authoring may transfer private files opaquely
on the owner's instruction. Never invoke its owner-only command on real data. No private creative material may
enter git, a story pack, agent context, logs or screenshots. These rules do not establish OS access control.
